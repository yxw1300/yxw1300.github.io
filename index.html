Destination 2D

Coupon → 300 mm wafer scale-up

From a research idea to a scalable process at a BEOL-compatible thermal budget.

Development arc

  1. 01

    Research idea

    A graphene heat spreader that forms inside the thermal budget of a finished chip’s back end, a window where conventional growth doesn’t work. The concept existed; a process didn’t.

  2. 02

    Coupon process

    Proved the mechanism on small coupons. Structured DOE across deposition, etch and anneal steps tied each process knob to film quality, coverage and adhesion, read out by Raman and XRD.

  3. 03

    150 mm wafers

    Moving to full wafers changed the problem from “does it grow” to “does it grow the same everywhere.” Cross-wafer uniformity, film stress and wafer bow became the metrics that mattered.

  4. 04

    300 mm transfer

    Redesigned the substrate holder and rebuilt the deposition recipe for the larger format, resolving the stress and uniformity issues that came with it.

Along the way

  • Data backbone

    Built internal data-acquisition software and a process–property database so every run is traceable and ready for AI-assisted analysis.

  • Foundry interface

    Served as the technical point of contact for foundry collaboration, aligning the film’s specifications with partner requirements.

Outcome

A research concept turned into a wafer-scale, BEOL-compatible process.

Destination 2D

Thermal test vehicle

Device fabrication and performance-data extraction for the integrated heat-spreader platform.

Workstream

  1. 01

    Model first

    Built an interfacial thermal-resistance model to predict what the film could deliver. Catching a flawed input in that model redirected development toward interface engineering.

  2. 02

    Device design

    Designed test structures that isolate in-plane heat spreading: an on-chip heater with resistive temperature sensors at increasing distances.

  3. 03

    Fabrication

    Patterned the structures in-house with e-beam lithography and RIE, alongside interdigitated-electrode, serpentine, via-chain and Hall-bar devices for electrical and interconnect characterization.

  4. 04

    Data extraction

    Calibrated the sensors, extracted thermal behavior, and validated the model against measured device performance.

Outcome

Development decisions grounded in a model checked against real devices.

Science Corporation

Full device fabrication

An end-to-end thin-film-to-dicing fabrication flow for a 4,000-channel electrode array.

Wafer fabrication

  1. Install & qualify
  2. Recipe development
  3. Troubleshooting
  4. Process control & training

Packaging

  1. 01

    Bumps

    Electroplated gold bumps for the flip-chip interconnect, tuning plating uniformity so every bump lands.

  2. 02

    Flip-chip & reflow

    Bonded the ASIC face-down onto the electrode array and reflowed, managing the thermal stress that step puts on the film stack.

  3. 03

    Wire bonding

    Connected the assembly out to the board.

  4. 04

    Epoxy molding

    Encapsulated with epoxy and polymer cures chosen to protect the bonds without cracking the films underneath.

Yield

  • Cracking during reflow

    Traced film cracking to CTE mismatch and thermal stress; adjusted cure and film stress to stop it.

  • Delamination

    Resolved metal-on-polyimide delamination through curing and surface-activation changes.

  • Test structures

    Designed on-glass structures for electrode, via/contact yield and accelerated lifetime testing, so problems showed up before devices.

Outcome

The full flow for a 4,000-channel neural recording device, from bare substrate to packaged die.

Science Corporation

Functional film development

Process development across the functional thin films in the device stack.

Film systems

  • SiC
  • SiN
  • AlN
  • Al₂O₃
  • TiN

Highlights

  • SiC encapsulation

    Developed and qualified a sputtered SiC encapsulation for an implantable package, validating chemical stability and durability through long-term accelerated lifetime testing.

  • TiN electrodes

    Developed sputtered TiN recipes for low-impedance recording electrodes.

  • Delamination root cause

    When SiC films delaminated, separated four candidate causes (intrinsic stress, thermal stress, CTE mismatch and chemical contamination) and fixed the process accordingly.

  • Dielectrics & barriers

    Developed the SiN, AlN and Al₂O₃ layers that insulate and protect the stack.

Outcome

Films that survive the body, not just the fab.

Science Corporation

Equipment ownership & sustainment

Tool troubleshooting, repair, operation, and sustainment for semiconductor process equipment.

Resolved faults

RF shorting in a CCP RIE

Symptom
The etcher could not run a stable plasma.
Root cause
A short in the RF path.
Fix
Located and repaired the short; returned the tool to service.

Sputter plasma extinguishing

Symptom
Plasma dropped out mid-deposition.
Root cause
Target overheating had degraded the magnetron magnets, weakening the confining field; confirmed with a gauss meter.
Fix
Replaced the degraded hardware and restored stable deposition.

Vacuum leaks

Symptom
Poor base pressure across several tools.
Root cause
Leaks at seals and fittings.
Fix
Leak-checked, resealed and requalified each chamber.

Outcome

Tools back in service, with root causes found rather than symptoms patched.
